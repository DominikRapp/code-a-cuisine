What is processed

When you request a recipe, the project processes the ingredients, quantities and preferences you enter or select. Technical request information is also processed to provide the feature and protect it from misuse.

Please do not enter names, contact details, health information or other personal information into ingredient fields.

Services used

Firebase stores recipe and request data. n8n runs the recipe workflow and checks the daily generation limit. Google Gemini may create a recipe when no suitable existing recipe is available.

Browser storage

Your browser stores liked recipe IDs and the displayed daily recipe availability locally. You can remove this information at any time by clearing this website’s site data in your browser settings.

Retention

Request and daily-limit records are kept only as long as needed to operate and protect the project. Generated recipes may remain in the shared recipe library until the project is taken offline.

Why data is processed

Data is processed to provide the recipe features you request and to maintain the secure operation of this temporary project.

Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion or restriction of your personal data. You may also object to processing. For requests, contact rapp.dominik@hotmail.com .

You may also lodge a complaint with the Austrian Data Protection Authority.
