Your ingredients

  • 80g Pasta noodles
  • 100g Baby spinach
  • 150g Cherry tomatoes
  • 1 piece Egg

Extra ingredients

  • 40g Parmesan cheese
  • 30ml Olive oil
  • Herbs dry basil, oregano, garlic

Directions

Hide directions Show directions
  1. 1.

    Cook the pasta

    Cook your noodles in boiling, salted water, until the pasta is al dente. Drain the pasta and reserve some of the pasta water.

  2. 2.

    Make the sauce

    While the pasta is cooking, heat olive oil in a pan over medium heat. Add the garlic, sauté until it starts to turn golden. Add the tomatoes, oregano, salt, and pepper, and cook for 3-4 minutes.

  3. 3.

    Finish the pasta

    Add the noodles to the sauce, then add pasta water until the sauce is the right consistency. Simmer for 1 minute, then add the spinach, basil, chili flakes, and parmesan.

  4. 4.

    Make the sauce

    Lower the heat to low, stir until mixed, and remove from the heat. Season to taste with parmesan cheese, and enjoy.
